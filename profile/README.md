### [Open Health Sync](https://github.com/soma-intelligence/open-health-sync)

An open source, self-hosted health data aggregator. It collects data from health apps and wearables, normalizes it to one model, and serves it back over REST, FHIR R4, Open mHealth, signed webhooks, CSV and Parquet, and a read-only MCP server so an AI agent can read your own data. One Go binary plus Postgres, and it runs on a Raspberry Pi. Website: [openhealthsync.com](https://openhealthsync.com).

[Quick start](https://github.com/soma-intelligence/open-health-sync#readme) · [Docs](https://github.com/soma-intelligence/open-health-sync/tree/main/docs) · [Releases](https://github.com/soma-intelligence/open-health-sync/releases) · [Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/soma-intelligence/open-health-sync/security)

### [OnePass](https://github.com/soma-intelligence/onepass)

An open source, post-quantum password manager for Apple devices. End to end encrypted, kept on your devices and in your own private iCloud. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6831411523) · [somalabs.me/onepass](https://somalabs.me/onepass)

### [Prism](https://github.com/soma-intelligence/prism)

An open source health app that scores whatever your watch, ring or iPhone has recorded in Apple Health. Free, no subscription, usable without an account. [somalabs.me/prism](https://somalabs.me/prism)

### [Soma Flight](https://github.com/soma-intelligence/Soma_Flight)

A flight tracker that follows your flights from booking to baggage claim, with Live Activities, widgets and a Watch app, and keeps a Passport of every trip you take.

### [ViaForm](https://github.com/soma-intelligence/ViaForm)

One app for 3D design and electronics. Design and Electronics are two workspaces of one project, saved together as a `.viaform` file.
